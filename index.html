Questions people ask us

How long does it take to open an account?

Filling in the online form takes about three minutes. After you upload your NID and a selfie, our team verifies your identity within 24 hours and you can deposit right away.

Do I need to visit a branch?

Not for a basic savings or student account. Current accounts for businesses, and some loans, need a one-time branch visit to sign documents.

What do I need to open an account?

A valid NID or smart card, a recent photo or selfie, your mobile number and email, and a nominee. Businesses also need a trade licence and TIN.

How do I reset my password?

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in panel. We send a one-time code to your registered mobile number. We never ask for your password or OTP by phone.

What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?

Freeze it instantly from Cards in your dashboard, then call the hotline at 16999 (24/7) to block and reissue it.

Does Asha Bank charge or pay interest?

No. Asha Bank operates strictly on a 0% interest model. We do not charge interest on loans, nor do we pay interest on deposits. We apply a transparent, nominal 0.001% annual fee for maintaining, storing, and vault-insuring your funds.

What are the fees?

Account opening, instant transfers between Asha Bank accounts and bill payments are free. For storing and maintaining money in the bank, a nominal safekeeping fee of 0.001% per year applies. Zero interest is charged.
